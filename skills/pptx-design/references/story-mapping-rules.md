# Story Mapping Rules

Use the indexed slide archetypes as reusable narrative moves.

## Default Sequence

1. Hero: the deck thesis or desired decision.
2. Agenda: use when the deck is a meeting or walking-deck narrative.
3. Executive summary: situation, implication, recommendation, and decision.
4. Problem statement: the highest-stakes challenge.
5. Proof or metrics: why the challenge is urgent or worth funding.
6. Transformation positioning: why the challenge is broader than one workstream.
7. Approach card grid or timeline: the solution model or service architecture.
8. Detail slides: bullets, keyword cards, two-column detail, case study, or differentiator grid.
9. Close or next step: use credentials, statement-photo, bullet-list, or section-divider layout depending on content.

## Archetype Selection

- `hero`: Use for the first slide only, unless building multiple chapter decks.
- `section-divider`: Use between chapters or before a major pivot.
- `problem-statement`: Use when the source content describes pain, risk, urgency, or market pressure.
- `transformation-positioning`: Use when reframing from technology work to business outcomes.
- `approach-card-grid`: Use for four pillars, phases, offers, capabilities, or value drivers.
- `statement-photo`: Use for a major point that needs visual weight.
- `bullet-list`: Use for dense details, recommendations, assumptions, or proof points.
- `keyword-card-grid`: Use for three pillars, audiences, findings, or capability groups.
- `timeline`: Use for four-step processes, waves, roadmap phases, or maturity progression.
- `differentiator-grid`: Use for two sets of three findings, differentiators, metrics, or client needs.
- `agenda`: Use for meeting flows, RFP response walkthroughs, or multi-section walking decks.
- `executive-summary`: Use for leader-friendly situation / implication / recommendation framing.
- `metric-proof`: Use when the source has 3-4 key statistics, outcomes, or quantified proof points.
- `two-column-detail`: Use for current/future state, option comparisons, before/after, or workstream comparison.
- `case-study`: Use for client proof, testimonial, or result stories with a supporting image.
- `credentials`: Use for West Monroe qualifications, team strengths, differentiators, or why-us sections.

## Duplication Guidance

Duplicate slides when content naturally has more items than a layout supports. Do not force seven bullets into a slide built for four. Preserve visual rhythm by alternating dense layouts with more open layouts.
